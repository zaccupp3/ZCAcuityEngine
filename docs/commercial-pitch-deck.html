Investor Audience

Acuity Engine

A healthcare operations software platform that helps hospitals build safer, fairer, and more defensible assignments while creating a repeatable subscription and expansion revenue model.

Clinical Ops

Balances patient assignments using staffing, acuity, discharge risk, continuity, report-source burden, and room spread.

Business Model

Implementation fee plus recurring subscription revenue, with optional enterprise rollout across a regional hospital footprint.

1
Why This Is Commercially Attractive

The pain is frequent, costly, and still under-digitized

  • Assignments are still often built on paper or in Excel while charge staff are being interrupted constantly.
  • That process increases the risk of hidden workload concentration, excess handoffs, and inconsistent shift-to-shift assignment quality.
  • The financial exposure is not only operational delay. It extends to turnover, burnout pressure, and avoidable hospital-acquired events.

Deployment Story

Fast enough to support enterprise expansion

Phase Typical duration What happens
Discovery and workflow mapping 2 to 3 weeks Define unit scope, staffing model, assignment rules, and rollout plan.
Configuration and training 2 to 4 weeks Set up users, tabs, workflow expectations, and go-live support model.
Operational go-live without deep integration 4 to 8 weeks total Fastest path when the unit can start with configured workflows and manual data entry.
Go-live with formal interface/integration work 10 to 14 weeks after kickoff, often inside a 4 to 6 month enterprise window Consistent with Redox integration guidance and health-system expectations for new connected technologies.

Why This Can Sell

Pricing sits well below plausible hospital value

  • A conservative modeled impact case from the earlier review was roughly $438K in annual hospital value from modest turnover and safety-event improvement.
  • That means a $96K ARR single-hospital pilot price can still sit well below a realistic operational value range.
  • At multi-hospital scale, the sales story becomes stronger because the product supports repeatable workflows, standardization, and shared system-level visibility.
Low-friction entry

Start as an operational workflow platform before full technical integration.

Expansion motion

Land in one unit or hospital, then expand to several sites in the same area or system.

7
Investor Takeaway

A practical path from pilot to regional footprint

  • Initial wedge: one-hospital pilot with implementation plus subscription.
  • Expansion path: regional rollout to 3 to 5 hospitals with standardized workflows.
  • System value: recurring ARR from a multi-hospital footprint plus optional enterprise services.
  • Go-live expectation: 4 to 8 weeks without deep integration, or roughly 10 to 14 weeks after kickoff when formal interface work is included.
