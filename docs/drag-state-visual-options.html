Drag State Visual Options

This review compares several ways to show RN/PCA tile reordering without making the selected card translucent. The goal is to preserve readability while still making it obvious that tiles can switch order.

What We Want

  • Full readability while dragging
  • Clear signal for where the card will land
  • Low visual noise on already busy staffing boards
  • Consistent feel across Live and Oncoming

Recommended Direction

  • Primary: Insertion rails on the hovered target
  • Secondary: Mild lift + outline on the dragged card
  • Append area: Dashed board outline when dropping into blank space

1. Lift + Outline

The dragged tile stays fully readable and looks picked up from the board.

Best when you want the board to feel premium and calm, but by itself it is weaker at showing the exact landing position.

2. Insertion Rails

The target card shows a blue bar on its top or bottom edge to indicate placement.

Best at answering “where exactly will this land?” with the least disruption to the board content.

4. Target Pulse Only

The dragged card stays almost unchanged while the hovered target edge animates subtly.

Best if you want the least visual clutter, but it is not quite as explicit as the rail system.

Recommended Implementation

Use Insertion Rails as the main signal, add a mild lift + outline to the dragged tile, and keep the existing dashed append-state around the board when a tile is headed into open space. That combination keeps the cards readable, clearly shows order changes, and fits a clinical board better than making the selected card translucent.
